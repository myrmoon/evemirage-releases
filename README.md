<p><strong>1.0.16</strong> — November 6, 2026</p>

<p align="center"><img src="assets/en/improvements.png" width="100%" alt="IMPROVEMENTS"></p>

<ul>
<li>Wallet: a "Gifts / Loans" button shows what you gave or received, player by player and corporation by corporation</li>
<li>Each exchange can be qualified (gift, loan, repayment; dues or settlement for a corporation) with a note, and a "Loans / Debts" table shows what you are owed and what you owe</li>
<li>Qualifications follow you across all your PCs through the sync folder</li>
</ul>

<details>
<summary>The last 5 previous versions</summary>

<p><strong>1.0.15</strong> — November 6, 2026</p>
<ul>
<li>The player count on Tranquility, at the bottom of the window, now refreshes every minute</li>
<li>Task Manager now simply shows "EveMirage"</li>
<li>Update window: the "Install now" button responds immediately instead of waiting for the startup reads to finish</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>1.0.14</strong> — November 6, 2026</p>
<ul>
<li>Dashboard: boosters now fit in one card ("5 / 6"), and each character has its own line with the bonus icon and a bar for the time left</li>
<li>Downtime now reads "DT 12:00 (EVE Time 11:00)", in your PC's time</li>
<li>D-Scan rebuilt</li>
<li>Corporation killboard and Corporation frame</li>
<li>Visual improvements</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>1.0.11</strong> — November 2, 2026</p>
<ul>
<li>ESI and market prices reread at launch; ESI at most once a minute, prices at most once an hour</li>
<li>Price pages reread their prices when opened</li>
<li>New icons for Navigation, Mapping, Intel and Routes; Navigation now follows Missions</li>
<li>Price rereads say why when there is nothing to price</li>
<li>The installers' publisher name matches the program's</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>1.0.10</strong> — November 1, 2026</p>
<ul>
<li>Passages: several maps, pilots shown on the map, structures read from the D-scan</li>
<li>New Navigation &gt; Routes page: a route with the heat of each system, Thera and Turnur, favourites and favourite routes</li>
<li>Planetary: extractors, production per product, Discord or Windows alerts, your choice</li>
<li>Visual improvements</li>
<li>The Windows notification no longer uses PowerShell (Norton blocked it)</li>
<li>Installer less suspicious to antivirus software</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>1.0.9</strong> — October 30, 2026</p>
<ul>
<li>Updates download into the program folder and are removed once installed</li>
<li>Translation fixes</li>
</ul>
</details>
