<p align="center">EveMirage gathers what an EVE Online pilot needs every day: skill plans for all your characters, missions, mining, industry, the market and your corporation's life. One window, connected to ESI, that keeps everything on your PC.</p>

<p><strong>1.1.6</strong> — November 10, 2026</p>

<p align="center"><img src="assets/en/improvements.png" width="100%" alt="IMPROVEMENTS"></p>

<ul>
<li>Corporation journal: every arrival and departure in the corporation is recorded, whether the member uses EveMirage or not</li>
<li>A new member appears on their real corporation join date</li>
<li>New read-only ESI permission (member list): a window offers to reconnect your characters at first launch</li>
</ul>

<details>
<summary>The last 5 previous versions</summary>

<p><strong>1.1.5</strong> — November 10, 2026</p>
<ul>
<li>Corporation journal: a card under the killboard on Corporation › General (new members, departures, co-leaders, awarded badges) and a searchable history</li>
<li>13 honorary distinctions, awarded by the corporation leader or a co-leader</li>
<li>30 corporation tenure badges</li>
<li>Member page redesigned: portrait, characters, badges</li>
<li>Character logout: the security copy of the tokens is deleted at the next launch</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>1.1.4</strong> — November 9, 2026</p>
<ul>
<li>**PC sync**: your wallet, reputations and mining from all your PCs come together through a Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox folder, set up in one click in Settings; your tokens never travel.</li>
<li>**Dashboard**: the activity period also offers 1 year, and the Standings card gathers every standing from all your characters with the net total, rises in green and drops in red.</li>
<li>**Visual improvements** on the dashboard.</li>
<li>**Security**: the update is checked more strictly before it runs, and sync, Discord webhooks, the forum and the error log are hardened.</li>
<li>**Missions**: the "Illegal activity" sheet states the real standing loss, and the compact list now reads in full.</li>
<li>**Performance**: screens open faster (Industry › Blueprints in particular) and switching themes is much quicker.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>1.1.3b</strong> — November 8, 2026</p>
<ul>
<li>Larger, clearer Ω / α badge on character cards</li>
<li>The left menu scrolls when a group opens in a short window: no more cut-off entries</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>1.1.3</strong> — November 8, 2026</p>
<ul>
<li>Standings as a market-style table: your favourite factions and corporations that moved, with their change over 24 h, 3 d, 7 d or 1 m</li>
<li>Clearer accelerators and character cards (Ω and α)</li>
<li>Faster launch, prices refreshed in the background with their progress next to the Price button</li>
<li>Pick a character without following it, "Restart" from the notification area, smoother typing in PvE Missions</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>1.1.2</strong> — November 8, 2026</p>
<ul>
<li>Dashboard: 1 d / 7 d / 30 d period selector for all activities (remembered between launches)</li>
<li>Dashboard: new Standings card, rises and falls like a stock ticker</li>
<li>Prices refresh in the background at launch, with an indicator at the bottom of the window</li>
<li>Faster launch and dashboard</li>
<li>Favorite corporations: standings values show as soon as the page opens, with no wait</li>
</ul>
</details>
